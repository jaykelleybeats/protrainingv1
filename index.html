DAILY SNAPSHOT

Automatic daily progress

Your first day starts at 0. When today's stats are available, post them once for the day. DropZone then compares today's posted stats with your previous posted day and adds the earned progress points to your total.

A positive number means more of the tracked stat than the previous snapshot. A negative number means it went down.

HOW THE SCORE WORKS

Daily progress points

Each stat that improves compared with your previous snapshot earns +1. Each stat that decreases earns -1. Unchanged stats earn 0. This is a simple progress indicator, not a skill rating.
